# Novelty Decision

Decision: STRONG_REVISE

The v5_expanded rebuild defines a narrower and more defensible novelty boundary than the archive draft and v4.1 package: retrieval-augmented control should retrieve by action-conditioned embodied mechanism compatibility, not by language, visual, or state-nearest similarity alone.

The local evidence supports this boundary against a broad comparator set, including conformal retrieval, learned expected utility retrieval, model-predictive retrieval arbitration, failure-aware active retrieval, and the retained v4 mechanism controller. The claim remains incomplete without real robot or accepted high-fidelity evidence.
