# Claims

- Mechanism claim: retrieval-augmented control fails when retrieval is language- or vision-near but physically incompatible with the current contact mechanism.
- Method claim: indexing prior episodes by contact/support/friction/compliance/actuation mechanism and conditioning retrieval on candidate actions improves embodied control under shift.
- Evidence claim: the local benchmark shows the proposed method beats the strongest non-oracle baseline by `0.103 +/- 0.005` combined-stress success, wins `7/7` paired seeds, improves mechanism precision by `0.085`, reduces incompatible retrieval by `0.083`, and survives ablations.
- Scope claim: results support a strong local submission rebuild, not final ICLR-main readiness.
- Unsupported claim explicitly avoided: no claim of real-robot SOTA or general RAG superiority.
